# 3D Signs


“3d” of course stands for “Three dimensional,” which means signs that have proportions. Our sign writers absolutely adore 3D signage because 3D (especially lighted signs) make your brand go WOW here in Sydney. 3D signage often relies on light, and as lightbox signs or other types of multi-dimensional signs, they bring attention to your business. 3D signs showcases your business and can propel your brand to the world. 3D signage can be produced from a wide variety of materials, and they are durable enough to last many years of use. Our design team can take a 3D sign concept and then work thru all your options and discuss what will work the best for your business.

With 3d signs, you have lots of options when it comes to adding light. Let there by light is a great mantra because light makes signs go WOW. Effects that you can add include adding shadows, halo lighting, or internal lighting. All of these can help make your signs noteworthy, and assist in getting your brand to the front. Signs are part of your brand, and the better they look, the better your business appears. In addition, 3D signage can be designed to give your business a certain amount of impact, and that can drive your message. Our 3D sign company is based in Sydney, but we work across all of Australia, from Sydney to Melbourne, Brisbane to Canberra, and everywhere in between.

The exact price of your 3d lettering will depend on how it is made and how large you need it. As many of our 3d signs are produced in a bespoke manner, there isn’t a precise formula for the cost. However, if you provide our design team with a budget, we can show you what the possibilities are for your sign. An important note is that a sign using quality materials will last longer, which can save you in the long run. For a clear quote on the total cost of your sign, give our team a call on 1300 448 608.

You can use our design team to help with preparing your signs. As a general rule, the simpler your design, the higher readability it has, and the better it will function. It can be fun to design a clever looking sign, but these are often costly to produce and hard to understand. With most signs, you need to get your message across in the shortest amount of time, and if someone needs to spend time working out what it says, they often won’t bother.

It is better to use illumination instead of complex designs when seeking to make your sign stand out. Our team can run you through all the options and suggest what style of signs are best for your purposes.
