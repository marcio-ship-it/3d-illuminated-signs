# Illuminated Signs


Illuminated signs are one of the best ways to showcase your brand to the world. Light is key to contemporary signage, whether it’s backlit signs, cabinet signs, LED signs, or the “new” LED Neon type of signage. At 3D Illuminated Signs, we have a team of sign designers and artisans ready to work on your brand and bring your vision to the light. Our sign team uses best-in-class light-based technology to create iluminated signs, and these illuminated signs are then made to your exact specifications. In addition, our design staff is available to install signage in any location all over Australia.

Illuminated signs are perfect for any type of light, whether that be the nature light of the day, the moonlight on a wonderful summer eve, or the dispersed light of Sydney at night. These types of signs can advertise your business at all times, and as people are naturally drawn to lighted signs, your business will stand out. In addition, our illuminated signs are low-maintenance and low voltage; this type of signage can transform your logo into an absolute showstopper. Your illuminated signs can be produced to match in with your existing brand colours and style. Illuminated signage can include many types of fonts, and the style can include built-up letters, raised lettering, projecting signs, and many others.

Illuminated signs are ideal for many businesses. Our sign writers can work with all sorts of businesses here in Sydney but also in Melbourne and Canberra. Our sign team has produced illuminated signs for government agencies, corporate events, small and medium businesses, and many others. In any space that you need to pass on a message, we can manufacture a suitable sign. Illuminated signs can provide a new look for an existing business or be the starting point to a complete rebrand.
