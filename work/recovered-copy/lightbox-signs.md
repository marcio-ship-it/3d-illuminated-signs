# Lightbox Signs


One of our specialties is the design and manufacturing of custom lightboxes. Lightbox signs have many uses in business, and they can be installed on the inside and outside of most buildings. Lightbox signs use lights and dimensions to make your signage go WOW.

We manufacture our signs in our Australian workshop from materials of the best quality. Lightbox signs are made with all the internal lighting installed, an aluminium and steel body (unless noted), with the panels being made from acrylic, polycarbonate, or a flex face banner. Any design or text of your choice can be printed on the lightbox sign. Such signs can be constructed to your specific measurements and the size you choose is generally only limited by your budget. If you’re looking for a lightbox sign, backlit sign or other type of advanced signage, call us today. Based in Sydney, we offer lightbox signage at competitive prices and can even serve Melbourne, Canberra, or throughout Australia.

Illuminated lightboxe signs are a fantastic method for displaying your business to the world at any time of the day or night. You can use traditional lettering or add in 3D lettering for an extremely professional look. The majority of lightboxes we produce use advanced LED technology as the lighting source. LEDs have many advantages over other lighting options, including a longer lifespan and not producing any noxious gases, and are cheaper to run; they are even a strong option for a reduced environmental impact.

## Applications



### Why Use Lightbox Signage?


With fierce competition in Sydney, you’ll want to make use of every possible advantage when it comes to signs and signage. This is where having a lightbox sign can help. A lightbox is visible at night, and this allows you to advertise your business through your signage at all times of the day and night. Also, as the lightbox can be double-sided, it does not matter which way the sign faces, it’ll be just as visible. Light attracts people, which is why you’ll see advertising lights used everywhere in major cities (especially if you ever visit amazing cities such as Las Vegas or New York!).

There is little doubt that many customers will decide on what they think of your business before you even get a chance to talk to them. When you have a professional sign, you are already breaking that first barrier, as it tells them you care about your business and makes it easy for them to find you. Signs tell them what your capabilities are. They are informational and they build your brand.


### Lightbox Signs: A Checklist


A lightbox sign should be in a position where it has the most impact and can be seen from a good distance. Standing out from the crowd is also ideal, and this is where a totally custom lightbox is going to fit in well. To get the most from your lightbox, we’d recommend the following:

- Have a good look at your business from all angles, think about where your current signs are visible from and what they may look like when you’re adding light. Will they perform better if moved? Review your business at night. What places may the lightbox perform best?

- What type of design do you like? Is your current logo modern or might it need a refresh? If you’re planning on updating any parts of your logo, doing it before organising a lightbox may be best. Consider hiring a designer for your lightbox, this will mean you can get the maximum value out of the sign.
